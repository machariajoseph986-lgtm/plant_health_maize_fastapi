WHY WE BUILT THIS PROJECT

Helping people access useful plant-health information

Plant diseases can reduce crop productivity, affect food security, and create challenges for farmers and other people involved in agriculture.

We developed this project to explore how artificial intelligence and a structured plant-health knowledge base can be combined to make disease information easier to access through a simple web application.

The system focuses its image-based diagnosis on maize, while the plant-health knowledge base provides information across multiple crops.
